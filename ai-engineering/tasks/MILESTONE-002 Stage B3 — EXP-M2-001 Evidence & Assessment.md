# 2. Assessment Discipline

The core rule is:

```text
Observed Fact
≠
Interpretation
≠
Conclusion
```

Use the following conceptual model:

```text
Observed Fact
        ↓
Evidence Interpretation
        ↓
Assessment
        ↓
Preliminary Outcome
```

Do not collapse these levels.

For every meaningful conclusion ask:

```text
What directly supports this?

What is inferred?

What remains uncertain?
```

---

# 3. Evidence Classification

Classify evidence into:

```text
Direct Observation

Repository Evidence

Human Intervention Evidence

Process Observation

Interpretive Evidence

Counterfactual Interpretation

Unknown / Unverified
```

For each major evidence item, document:

```text
Evidence

Type

Source

What it directly supports

What it does NOT prove
```

Do not invent evidence.

Do not convert absence of failure into evidence of success.

# 5. Positive Evidence Analysis

Identify genuine positive evidence.

Potential categories include:

```text
Boundary Clarity

Scope Control

Traceability

Reasoning Structure

Revision Discipline

Stop Discipline

Unexpected Problem Detection
```

For every positive signal, ask:

```text
Was this directly observed?

Could ordinary engineering judgment
have produced the same result?

Is the asset procedure plausibly associated
with the observed outcome?

What cannot be concluded?
```

Use cautious language such as:

```text
Observed

Associated With

Consistent With

May Indicate
```

Avoid:

```text
Proved

Guaranteed

Caused
```

unless directly justified.

---

# 6. Boundary Value Analysis

Perform a focused analysis of the experiment's strongest possible value signal:

```text
Boundary Discipline
```

The experiment encountered:

```text
A Potential Optional Historical Postscript
```

but the revision remained:

```text
Primary Target Only
```

Analyze:

```text
What was directly observed?

What role did the explicit boundary play?

Was scope expansion actually attempted?

Was scope expansion merely possible?

Could ordinary judgment alone have prevented it?
```

Do NOT claim:

```text
CANDIDATE-001 prevented scope creep
```

unless causality is directly supported.

A more defensible formulation may be:

```text
The explicit revision-boundary procedure
was associated with maintaining
a narrow modification scope.
```

But assess the exact evidence before using any conclusion.


Conceptual categories may include:

```text

Potential Redundancy

Unclear
```

Do NOT use numerical scoring.

Do NOT assign maturity points.

Important:

```text
One experiment
does not justify permanent procedure removal.
```

If a step appears redundant:

```text
Record the signal.
```

Do not redesign the asset yet.


# 11. Adaptation Analysis

Analyze every procedure adaptation.

For each adaptation determine:

```text
Why adaptation occurred

Was adaptation permitted?

Was the procedure too rigid?

Was the task unusual?

Was the adaptation normal engineering judgment?

Does the adaptation reveal a design ambiguity?
```

Do not assume:

```text
Adaptation
=
Design Failure
```

But do not dismiss it either.
